OES model

OES is the visual and effect language: tokens, visual rules, responsiveness, animation, and safe layout behavior.

Native syntax

EN

OES keeps CSS power but authors it with OFS-flavored words such as `gem`, `paint`, `fill`, `ink`, `curve`, `space`, `veil`, and `pulse`.

theme.oes
gem accent "#29e0bd"
paint .button
  fill "$accent"
  curve "8px"
veil "(max-width: 700px)"
  paint .grid
    grid-cols "1fr"
pulse "enter"
  rise
    opacity "0"
  rest
    opacity "1"
